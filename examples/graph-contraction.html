Edge contraction

Contract AB into A. Vertex B and edge AB disappear. The two other edges incident to B reconnect to A.

Edge labels are IDs. BC and BD keep their labels as they change endpoints. AC and BC run in parallel after the contraction.

Yellow marks the selected edge and its endpoints. Purple marks the redirected edges. Green shows A and the two redirected edges in the final result.
