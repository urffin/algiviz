Linked list · insert, remove, reverse

Start with 7 → 3 → 9. Insert 5 after 7, remove 3, then reverse the remaining list.

Follow the arrows from head to null. Each node has a value on the left and a next slot on the right. Yellow highlights changed links. Green marks the final chain.

Next and Previous step through the edits. Restart restores the original chain.

Each edit records a complete, valid chain. While the arrows animate, the renderer uses the old and new snapshots together.
