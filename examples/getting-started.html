Your first Player

A generator supplies four positions: 40 → 200 → 360 → 560. Your callback draws the circle; AlgiViz provides the controls.

Press Start to draw the first position, then Next state to move the circle. One more click after the last position detects completion. Restart creates a fresh generator and shows the first position.
