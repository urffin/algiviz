Binary tree · insert 1 and rotate right

Start with root 3 and its left child 2. Insert 1 by following the search order, then make one right rotation at 3. The final root is 2, with 1 on the left and 3 on the right.

Detailed tree traversal: custom objects, pointer, Canvas and SVG

Yellow: current comparison or rotation. Grey: inserted leaf during the rotation. Dashed squares: empty child slots. Green: the complete resulting tree.

Use Next and Previous to inspect the insertion, the rotation and the green result. Full history also lets you seek through the transition. Restart returns to the two-node tree.

This fixed left-left case illustrates one balancing operation, not a full AVL insertion algorithm. The in-order sequence remains 1 → 2 → 3 across the rotation. A different insertion may need another rotation or none.

This example needs the local build: npm 1.1.0 does not export /tree or /canvas/tree yet. The algorithm belongs to the example; the library supplies immutable tree steps, layout, drawing and Player controls.
